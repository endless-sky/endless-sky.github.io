v0.11.4 is now live on Github, Steam, and GoG! And shortly on Flathub and our Homebrew tap.

This release is being sent out to everyone, so you don't need to be on the beta branch to get it. This update focuses on bug fixes for the recent v0.11.3 release, so if you haven't played that version, then take a look at our previous announcement, or check the [changelog](https://github.com/endless-sky/endless-sky/blob/master/changelog) to see what's new.

For players that ran into issues with missions in 0.11.3:
- Return to Meligara in the Ritilas system to continue the new Ka'het missions.
- The "Destroy sensor probes" mission from the Successors will clear itself from your active missions the next time you land on Qasa-Sija-Iri.
- Remnant ships will no longer remain perpetually cloaked, so you can go back to finishing those escort missions.

A reminder that we have a feedback box for each major release; here is the one for [v0.11.3](https://forms.gle/LXDnqXqacBC4cfoU9). If you haven't looked at it already, it would help to check it out and leave us any feedback.

Also, if you haven't already, head over to [Github](https://github.com/endless-sky/endless-sky) and star Endless Sky or give us a positive review on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/404410/Endless_Sky/) or [GOG](https://www.gog.com/en/game/endless_sky) to help the game become more discoverable!

Our next unstable release, v0.11.5, is planned for late January of 2027. See you in the new year!
