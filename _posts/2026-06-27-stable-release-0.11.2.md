v0.11.2 is now live on Github, Steam, and GoG!

This release is being sent out to everyone, so you don't need to be on the beta branch to get it. This update focuses on bug fixes for the recent v0.11.0 and v0.11.1 releases, so if you haven't played either of those, then take a look at our previous announcements for those updates above, or check the [changelog](https://github.com/endless-sky/endless-sky/blob/master/changelog) to see what's new.

A reminder that we have a feedback box for each major release; here are the ones for [v0.11.0](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScl3EVWbqL3puI3poCQD7tVMCNBun76vlSyCTe9UoxW8eqM2A/viewform) and [v0.11.1](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfa9ureH8f_skQLHdk-Clg7y61yW22hXcH7xb1Gaqf856-I7g/viewform). If you haven't looked at them already, it would help to check them out and leave us any feedback.

Also, if you haven't already, head over to [Github](https://github.com/endless-sky/endless-sky) and star Endless Sky or give us a positive review on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/404410/Endless_Sky/) or [GOG](https://www.gog.com/en/game/endless_sky) to help the game become more discoverable!

Our next unstable release, v0.11.3, is planned for late September.
